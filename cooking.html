Cooking

Cooking is a creative hobby that lets me make something tasty while learning new techniques and flavours. I enjoy trying different recipes and improving my kitchen skills.

What I Love About Cooking

For me, cooking is more than just preparing meals—it's a form of self-expression and mindfulness. There's something satisfying about transforming raw ingredients into something delicious and sharing it with friends and family. I'm drawn to the precision required in baking and the improvisation that comes with stir-fries and sauces.

My Cooking Journey

I started cooking out of necessity during college, but it quickly became a passion. What began with simple pasta dishes has evolved into experimenting with cuisines from around the world. I've learned that cooking is as much about understanding why techniques work as it is about following recipes.

Favorite Cuisines

I'm particularly interested in Asian cuisine, especially Thai and Japanese cooking, where flavor balancing and fresh ingredients are paramount. I also enjoy Italian cooking for its emphasis on quality ingredients and timeless techniques. Recently, I've been exploring fusion cooking—combining elements from different cuisines to create unique dishes.

Kitchen Adventures

My kitchen is my laboratory. Whether I'm perfecting homemade pasta from scratch, experimenting with fermentation, or trying to replicate a restaurant dish, I love the challenge of mastering new culinary skills. Cooking has taught me patience, attention to detail, and the rewarding feeling of creating something with my hands.
